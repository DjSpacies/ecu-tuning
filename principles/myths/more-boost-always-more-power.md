# Myth: a higher boost number always means more power

## Claim
A boost figure says nothing without pressure ratio, temperature and turbine conditions. Past a turbo's efficient range, more boost can bring less air, more heat and less knock margin.

## Why (derivation)
Air mass depends on density, which falls with temperature. Compressor efficiency falls away from its island, heat rises, and a smaller turbine raises exhaust back pressure. Knock margin often runs out first.

## Assumptions
Turbocharged or supercharged engine.

## Breaks when
Within the compressor's efficient range, power does generally follow boost.

## Practical use
Compare airflow and charge temperature, not gauge numbers.

## Worked example
Two turbos at the same 200 kPa may have very different power, since one is working well outside its efficient range.
