# Pre-tune checklist

## Claim
A car should be mechanically and electrically sound before tuning starts. Many problems on the dyno were present before it arrived, and tuning cannot fix them.

## Why (derivation)
Fuel starvation, leaks, weak charging and bad compression all masquerade as tuning problems, and leaks at load are dangerous.

## Assumptions
Access to do these checks.

## Breaks when
A check made with the engine in a different condition will mislead.

## Practical use
Run the checklist first, record results and stop if something fails.

## Decision rules
Start with a full tank of the fuel to be tuned on. Look for oil, fuel and coolant leaks, and treat those as stops. Check base timing yourself, whoever says it is right. Do compression and leak-down tests from identical conditions. Start with the simple explanations when diagnosing.

## Data gates
Coolant, oil and fuel levels verified; battery and charging healthy.

## Worked example
A misfire under boost turns out to be a coolant leak onto a coil. Finding it first saves the session.
