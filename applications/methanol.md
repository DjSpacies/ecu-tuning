# Methanol profile

## Claim
Methanol needs about 2.3 times the fuel mass of gasoline, cools the charge strongly, and is hard to start cold. The fuel system is the limiting concern.

## Why (derivation)
Its low stoichiometric ratio and high latent heat follow from the composition. It attacks some materials and is toxic.

## Assumptions
The fuel system is rated for methanol.

## Breaks when
Cold starting and oil dilution are serious problems, and water absorption changes properties.

## Practical use
Size injectors and pump first. Handle with care, and check every wetted material.

## Worked example
An engine using 400 cc/min of gasoline at a point needs roughly 400 x 2.27 x 745/792 = 855 cc/min of methanol.
