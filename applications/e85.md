# E85 and ethanol blend profile

## Claim
E85 needs about 1.5 times the fuel mass of gasoline. Its octane and cooling usually give substantial knock margin, and the actual ethanol content varies.

## Why (derivation)
See the stoichiometry and energy entries. Cold-start needs extra fuel because ethanol evaporates poorly at low temperature.

## Assumptions
The blend is measured or known.

## Breaks when
Seasonal blends can be well below 85 percent ethanol. Fuel system capacity is the first thing to fail.

## Practical use
Check injector duty and pump capacity first. Treat a move to E85 as a new tune, not a fuel table scale.

## Worked example
An injector at 60 % duty on gasoline is near 86 % on E85 at the same lambda.
